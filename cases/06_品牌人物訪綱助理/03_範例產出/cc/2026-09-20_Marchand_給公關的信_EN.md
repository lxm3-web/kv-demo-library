**Questions**

1. From 2012 to 2018 at ATELIER NOVE you did one thing—leather. The "Fold" line ended up at 23% of their leather revenue. Looking back, what did that line teach you that you're still using now as Creative Director?
2. You've said, "I don't design for this season, I design what someone still wants to carry ten years from now." Is the detachable handle on the Ligne bag the first concrete answer to that sentence—or did the problem come first, and the sentence after?
3. In 2025 your repair service handled 42,000 pieces, up 35%. For a house that lives on selling new product, is that good news or a warning sign?
4. Relouve reports the Ligne bag holds 82% of its value on the resale market. When you designed it, were you thinking about its second owner at all?
5. You often talk about "the hand of the atelier craftsman." Is there one person in the workshop who changed what that phrase means to you? Just tell me one thing they did.
6. In Tokyo you said, "Asian customers ask the sharpest questions—what happens when this handle breaks? I love that." Now you're in Taipei: what's a sharper question you're hoping someone asks you?
7. 48% of your Asian customers are under 35. How do you make "something you'll use for ten years" land with a 25-year-old walking into the Xinyi store for the first time?
8. You took over in March; your first full collection is SS27. I'm not asking about the collection—I'm asking about the year in between. What's the first way of working you changed? And what did you deliberately decide not to touch yet?
